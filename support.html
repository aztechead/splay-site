Splay for the iPhone Duo

What you need

An iPhone Duo running iOS 27 or later. Splay works on the front screen in portrait. On the inner screen, and on other iPhones, it leaves pages alone.

Turning it on

  1. Open Settings, then Apps, then Safari, then Extensions.
  2. Tap Splay and turn on Allow Extension.
  3. Set All Websites to Allow.

If you would rather not allow every site, set All Websites to Ask and allow Splay on the sites you want.

Turning it off for one site

On that site, tap the page menu button at the left of Safari's address bar, then tap Splay. Tap it again to turn Splay back on. Splay remembers your choice for that site.

Text styles

Open the Splay app to choose how text sits next to Safari's buttons:

  • Justify and hyphenate: every line ends at the buttons and long words split across lines.
  • Justify: every line ends at the buttons without splitting words, so the gaps between words can get wider.
  • Ragged: text wraps normally and ends within a word of the buttons.

The style applies to every site from the next page you open.

If a page doesn't look right

  • Check that All Websites is set to Allow, or that Splay is allowed on that site.
  • Reload the page. If you change Safari's page zoom while a page is open, it needs a reload to fit again.
  • Some sites still won't fit perfectly. Turn Splay off for that site from the page menu.
