Privacy Policy

Splay · Last updated October 2026

Splay doesn't collect any data about you, and nothing it does sends information off your iPhone. It has no accounts and doesn't use analytics or advertising.

What the extension does with web pages

When you allow Splay in Safari, it runs on the pages you open and changes their layout for the iPhone Duo's front screen. Safari describes this permission as being able to "read and alter webpages and see browsing history". To resize a page, Splay looks at how the page is laid out. It doesn't save anything about the page, and it doesn't record which pages you visit. All of this happens inside Safari on your iPhone.

What is stored on your iPhone

Splay saves the sites you've turned it off for, in Safari's storage for the extension, and the text settings you picked in the Splay app. Both stay on your iPhone, and deleting the Splay app removes them.

Third parties

Splay doesn't include any third-party code or services.

Children

Splay doesn't collect data from anyone, children included.
