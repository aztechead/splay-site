Privacy Policy

Splay · Last updated October 2026

Splay does not collect any data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no servers.

What the extension does with web pages

When you allow Splay in Safari, it runs on the pages you open so it can change their layout for the iPhone Duo's front screen. Safari describes this permission as being able to "read and alter webpages and see browsing history". Splay only uses it to adjust how pages are laid out. It does not read, save or send what is on a page or which pages you visit. All of this happens inside Safari on your iPhone.

What is stored on your iPhone

Splay remembers two things, and both stay on your device:

  • The sites you have turned Splay off for, kept in Safari's storage for the extension.
  • The text style you picked in the Splay app.

Deleting the Splay app removes both.

Third parties

Splay contains no third-party code or services.

Children

Splay does not collect data from anyone, including children.
